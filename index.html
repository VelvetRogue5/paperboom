Three quick steps

How to Play

1. Tap to fire

Launch a ball from the cannon and let momentum, angles, and gravity do the work.

2. Break the structure

Hit weak points to send cans, boxes, stones, ice, jars, and blockers crashing down.

3. Clear the stage

Remove every required target before the ball counter reaches zero, then advance to the next level.

FAQ

Do I need a login?

No. Progress, coins, lives, inventory, and settings are stored locally on your device.

Does Paper Boom! include ads or tracking?

No. The current version does not show ads and does not include analytics, attribution, or tracking SDKs.
