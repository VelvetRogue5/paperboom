Description

Step right up and make it boom!

Paper Boom! is a handcrafted physics puzzle game where every shot can bring the whole stage crashing down. Tap to fire the cannon, find the weakest point, and smash colorful towers before you run out of balls.

Each level builds a new paper-craft challenge from cans, boxes, ice, stones, jars, blockers, and moving platforms. Aim carefully, use gravity and chain reactions, and watch every impact scatter the stage in a satisfying burst.

Features:
- 1,000 physics-based puzzle levels
- Simple one-tap cannon controls
- Colorful handcrafted paper-craft style
- Breakable cans, boxes, ice, stones, jars, and more
- Moving, rotating, and multi-stage layouts
- Limited-ball challenges that reward smart shots
- Rocket boosters for difficult structures
- Lives, coins, rewards, and win streaks
- No account required
- Free to play, supported by ads
- Optional rewarded videos for extra shots
- Progress stored locally on your device

Line up the shot, start the chain reaction, and clear the circus stage with a boom.

App Store Review Notes Draft

  • Account required: No.
  • In-app purchases: No active purchase processing in the current build.
  • User-generated content: No.
  • Player communication: No.
  • Contains ads: Yes. Rewarded video and interstitial ads via AppLovin MAX, mediating AppLovin, Unity Ads, Vungle (Liftoff), Mintegral, Pangle (ByteDance), BidMachine, and Bigo Ads. Banner format is present but disabled by default. iOS ad units may not be live in every build.
  • Third-party SDKs: AppLovin MAX and mediation adapters; Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics, and Remote Config.
  • Tracking: This build does not present the App Tracking Transparency prompt and does not access the IDFA. Ads and measurement use an app-generated, keychain-backed device identifier.
  • Data posture: Game progress and preferences are stored locally. Gameplay, ad, and crash events plus install and ad-revenue reports are sent with the device identifier. Support email is user-initiated.

App Privacy Label Draft

Draft answers for the App Store Connect App Privacy questionnaire, to be confirmed against the production archive:

  • Identifiers · Device ID — collected, used for Third-Party Advertising, Analytics, and App Functionality. Not linked to a user identity.
  • Usage Data · Product Interaction and Advertising Data — collected for Analytics, Third-Party Advertising, and Product Personalization. Not linked to a user identity.
  • Diagnostics · Crash Data and Performance Data — collected for App Functionality and Analytics. Not linked to a user identity.
  • Contact Info · Email Address — only if the player chooses to send a support email; used for App Functionality (Customer Support).
  • Not collected: name, precise location, contacts, photos, health, financial info, browsing history, purchases (no active IAP in this build).

Because ad networks receive a device identifier for advertising purposes, confirm with current App Store rules whether the app must declare "Used for Tracking" and present the App Tracking Transparency prompt before this build is submitted. The app does not present that prompt today.

Connectivity and Privacy Note

Core gameplay works without an account and stores progress locally, but the app requires network access to load ads, fetch remote configuration, and send analytics, crash, and install-measurement reports. The current iOS build does not include active App Store purchase processing.
