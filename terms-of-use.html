5. In-App Purchases

Paper Boom! offers optional in-app purchases through the App Store. You never need to buy anything to play or finish the game. Current offers include:

They are not personal property. Consumable items are added to the game save on your device when the purchase completes, and are used up as you play.

Because progress is stored only on your device, consumable items you have bought, like the rest of your progress, cannot be restored if you delete the app, clear its data, or change devices.
