Paper Boom! for Android

This policy explains how Paper Boom! handles information on Android. You still do not need an account, and your game progress is saved on your device. The app now also shows ads, reports gameplay and performance data to analytics and crash-reporting services, and measures installs so we can understand how the game is found and how it performs. Optional in-app purchases are processed by Google Play.

Information Handled by the App

Paper Boom! does not require an account and does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, precise location, photos, contacts, microphone, camera, or payment card details.

To provide the game, Paper Boom! stores game state and preferences on your device. This may include your current level, session progress, coins, lives and timers, rocket inventory, streak and tutorial state, music, sound and haptic settings, purchase-related in-game inventory, and technical state needed to keep local saves consistent.

The app generates a random device identifier the first time it runs and stores it in the app's own storage. It is not derived from your identity, your Google Account, or the Android Advertising ID. This identifier is sent with the analytics, crash-reporting, advertising, and measurement data described below so that events from the same installation can be counted together. It is removed when you clear the app's storage or uninstall the app, unless Android restores the app's data from a backup.

The app also uses identifiers provided by Android and by its measurement partners:

  • Android Advertising ID. A resettable identifier provided by Google Play services. The AppLovin, AppsFlyer, and Meta SDKs in the app may read it for advertising and measurement, as described below. The app does not send it to our own measurement endpoint. If you have deleted your Advertising ID, or your device has no Google Play services, no Advertising ID is available.
  • AppsFlyer ID and Meta anonymous ID. Random identifiers created by the AppsFlyer and Meta SDKs inside the app. They are used by those services and are also included in our own measurement reports so the records can be matched.

The app also creates a separate random support identifier and stores it on your device. If you open support, the identifier may be placed in the subject of a draft email. It leaves your device only if you choose to send that email.

Along with the identifiers above, the services described below receive technical information about your device and the app: device model and manufacturer, Android version, screen size, app version and version code, language and region settings, time zone, store channel, network connection status, and the IP address your device uses to reach the internet.

Analytics and Crash Reporting

Paper Boom! uses Google Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics. Analytics records gameplay and app lifecycle events so we can see how the game is actually played and where players get stuck. Crashlytics records crashes and unhandled errors so we can fix them.

The events we send describe gameplay, not you personally. They cover:

  • App and session activity: app opens, session start and end, session length, screens viewed.
  • Level activity: level start, level end and result, retries, continues, and tutorial progress.
  • In-game economy: coins earned and spent, lives consumed, streaks, gift boxes, and booster usage.
  • Shop and purchase activity: shop opens, product taps, and purchase results.
  • Settings activity: settings opened, closed, and toggled.
  • Ad activity: ad requests, loads, impressions, failures, closes, and rewarded-ad completions.
  • Experiment activity: which configuration or A/B group your installation received.
  • Delivery diagnostics: whether our own events and reports reached our servers, and how late they were.

We also set a small number of properties about the installation, such as current level, last completed level, last session length, level pack version, a coarse coin-balance range, and the build channel and build number. Crash reports include the device identifier, device and OS details, app version, and a technical stack trace.

Google processes this data as our service provider under the Firebase privacy documentation and the Google Privacy Policy.

Advertising and the Advertising ID

Paper Boom! includes in-app advertising and is monetized primarily through ads. The app can show rewarded video ads that you choose to watch in exchange for an in-game reward, and interstitial ads that appear between levels. A banner format also exists in the app and is disabled by default; if we enable it, ads may also appear at the bottom of the gameplay screen. Ad placement, frequency, and cooldowns are controlled by remote configuration and can change without an app update.

Ads are delivered through AppLovin MAX, which mediates demand from several advertising networks. The networks currently integrated in the app are AppLovin, Unity Ads, Vungle (Liftoff), Mintegral, Pangle (ByteDance), BidMachine, and Bigo Ads. Depending on your version, some of these networks may not be enabled, but the software components ship with the app.

To select, deliver, and measure ads, these providers process device and usage information. This typically includes the Android Advertising ID, which their SDKs may access for advertising and measurement purposes, together with the app's own device identifier described above, IP address and the coarse location derived from it, device model and Android version, network and locale information, and your interactions with the ads shown. We do not send them your game saves or your support email. AppLovin also reports estimated ad revenue for each impression back to the app, which we use for the measurement described in the next section.

Each advertising network handles the data it receives as an independent controller under its own privacy policy, including AppLovin and Unity. AppLovin publishes the current list of its mediation and demand partners on its privacy page.

Install Measurement and Attribution

Paper Boom! uses three measurement services so that we can count installs, understand which advertising campaign or marketing channel a player came from, and measure how much advertising revenue an installation generates in aggregate.

AppsFlyer. The AppsFlyer SDK starts when the app launches. It receives the app-generated device identifier, the AppsFlyer ID, the Android Advertising ID where available, the Google Play Install Referrer (which tells it which Play Store link or campaign led to the install), IP address, device and app information, the gameplay, shop, and purchase events described under Analytics, and the estimated revenue of each ad impression. AppsFlyer matches this against ad campaigns to attribute the install, and processes the data as our service provider under the AppsFlyer Services Privacy Policy.

Meta (Facebook) App Events. The Meta SDK records app activation and a limited set of gameplay, ad, and purchase events so that we can measure installs from ads we run on Facebook and Instagram. It receives the Meta anonymous ID, the Android Advertising ID where available, device and app information, and IP address. The app does not use Facebook Login and does not access your Facebook account. Meta handles this data under the Meta Privacy Policy.

Our measurement endpoint. The app also reports installation and gameplay milestones to our own endpoint at app-d.paperboom.com.ai, operated for us. On first launch the app sends an install report containing the device identifier, AppsFlyer ID, Meta anonymous ID, app id, platform, app version and version code, store channel and region, device model and manufacturer, Android version, screen size, language, country, and time zone. After that, selected events — app opens, tutorial completion, ad impressions, and cumulative ad revenue — are sent with the same identifiers. Purchase events are not sent to this endpoint.

These reports are queued on your device when the network is unavailable and retried later, so a report may be delivered hours or days after the event happened. Queued reports are stored on your device until they are delivered or expire.

Remote Configuration and Experiments

The app uses Firebase Remote Config to fetch settings such as ad frequency, level pacing, and shop availability, and to run A/B tests. Fetching configuration sends the device identifier and basic app and device information to Google, and your installation may be assigned to a test group. The group you are in is recorded as an analytics event so results can be compared. If configuration cannot be fetched, the app falls back to the values built into the release.

Your Choices

  • Rewarded ads are optional. You choose whether to watch one. Interstitial ads are shown automatically between levels; buying Remove Ads turns them off.
  • Advertising ID controls. In Android Settings you can reset your Advertising ID, or delete it so apps receive no advertising identifier at all, under Settings › Privacy › Ads (the exact path varies by device). Once it is deleted, AppLovin, AppsFlyer, and Meta no longer receive it.
  • Notifications. You can decline the notification permission, turn reminders off in the game's settings, or turn off notifications for Paper Boom! in Android Settings.
  • Clearing app data. Clearing the app's storage or uninstalling Paper Boom! removes your local game saves, including coins and items you bought, and the app's device identifier. Because there is no cloud save, this also removes your progress permanently. Remove Ads can be restored with Restore Purchases.
  • Requests about your data. Email us to ask about, or request deletion of, data associated with your device identifier. It helps if you include the support identifier from the in-game support email so we can find the right records.

Notifications

Paper Boom! can show local reminder notifications, such as a short message around midday or in the evening inviting you back to play. They are scheduled on your device for up to 14 days ahead, avoid late-night quiet hours, and are rescheduled each time you open the game. They are not sent from a push server, and no notification token is created or shared.

On Android 13 and later, the app asks for the notification permission, by default after you win a level. The app records in analytics whether the permission prompt was shown and your answer, and when reminders are scheduled, shown, or opened.

Android Permissions and Haptics

Paper Boom! may use Android's vibration capability to provide optional haptic feedback for taps, impacts, warnings, and wins. This capability does not provide us with personal information. You can turn haptics off in the game's settings.

The app also uses these permissions: network access to load ads, fetch configuration, and send the reports described above; the notification permission for local reminders; permission to run after the device restarts so scheduled reminders can be restored; and the Google Play billing permission for in-app purchases. Bundled advertising and measurement components declare the Advertising ID permission. The current version does not request access to precise location, contacts, photos, microphone, or camera.

Google Play Purchases

Paper Boom! offers optional in-app purchases — coin packs, bundles, a Starter Pack, and Remove Ads — which are processed by Google Play. Google handles your payment method, Google Account, and billing details under the Google Privacy Policy. We never receive your name, Google Account email, or payment card details.

To complete a purchase, Google Play tells the app the product identifier, the purchase state, an order identifier, and a purchase token. The app uses these on your device to acknowledge or consume the purchase with Google Play and grant the item, and does not send the purchase token to our own servers. Purchased coins, boosters, and lives are stored in your local game save, and the app records on your device whether you have bought Remove Ads or the Starter Pack. When the app starts, it checks Google Play for purchases that were paid but not yet delivered, and Restore Purchases asks Google Play for your Remove Ads purchase.

We record each purchase outcome as a purchase_result event containing the product identifier, the result (such as purchased, restored, cancelled, or failed), and the order identifier or the reason it did not complete, such as a payment that is still pending. It does not contain the price you paid. Like other gameplay events, it is sent with the identifiers described above to Firebase Analytics, to AppsFlyer, and to Meta, so that we can measure which ad campaigns bring paying players. Shop opens and product taps are also recorded as analytics events. After a completed purchase, your installation is marked as a purchaser on your device, which can change the offers you see and turn off interstitial ads.

Support and External Links

When you choose Support, Paper Boom! can open an email app with a draft addressed to us. We receive only the information you decide to include and send, together with the support identifier in the subject. Your email provider processes the message under its own terms.

The app may also open legal pages in your browser, and tapping an ad will open a destination chosen by the advertiser. Once you leave the app, the privacy practices of the destination website and browser apply.

How Information Is Used

Local game data is used to resume progress, maintain balances and timers, and remember tutorials and settings. Local data also records in-game content granted after a completed Google Play purchase, and whether Remove Ads is active.

Analytics, crash, advertising, configuration, and measurement data are used to operate and improve the game: to fix crashes and bugs, understand which levels are too hard or too easy, balance the in-game economy, deliver and cap ads, measure ad performance and revenue, run A/B tests, count installs and attribute them to ad campaigns, remind you to play if you allow notifications, and detect invalid or fraudulent activity.

Information you voluntarily send to support is used to answer your request, troubleshoot issues, and protect the game and its players from abuse.

Sharing and Sale of Information

We do not sell or rent player personal information, and we do not share your game saves or support correspondence with advertisers. Data is shared only as described in this policy: with Google for analytics, crash reporting, and remote configuration; with AppLovin and the mediated advertising networks to deliver and measure ads; with AppsFlyer, Meta, and our measurement endpoint to count installs, attribute them to ad campaigns, and measure revenue, including purchase results with AppsFlyer and Meta; with Google to process Google Play purchases; when you choose to contact support; when required by law; or when necessary to protect rights, safety, and security.

Some privacy laws treat sharing advertising identifiers with advertising networks for personalized advertising as a "sale" or "share" of personal information. The simplest way to stop Advertising ID-based sharing is to delete your Advertising ID as described in Your Choices. If you would also like us to stop sharing the app-generated device identifier for your device, contact us at the address below with that identifier.

Retention and Deletion

Local game data remains on your device until the app overwrites it, you clear the app's storage, or you uninstall the app. Paper Boom! currently does not provide cloud save, so clearing storage or uninstalling may permanently remove local progress. Queued measurement reports are held on your device for up to seven days and are discarded if they cannot be delivered.

Analytics, crash, and measurement data are retained by us and our providers only as long as needed for the purposes above, according to each provider's retention settings and applicable law, and are then deleted or aggregated so that they no longer identify a single installation.

Support emails are retained only as reasonably necessary to respond, keep support records, resolve disputes, and meet legal obligations. You may contact us to request deletion of information you previously provided through support, subject to applicable law.

Security

We use reasonable safeguards appropriate to the information handled by the app. Reports to our measurement endpoint are signed and sent over the network in the format that endpoint requires. No storage or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure, so avoid including sensitive personal information in support messages unless necessary.

Children's Privacy

Paper Boom! is a general-audience puzzle game. It is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The game has no accounts and no social features, but it does include advertising, gameplay analytics, and install measurement as described above, so it is not intended for use by children under the age at which consent is required in your country. If you believe a child has used the app or sent personal information through support, contact us so we can review and delete it where appropriate.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy when Paper Boom! or legal requirements change. The latest version will be posted here with a revised date. If a future release adds cloud saves, accounts, or another data service, this policy will be updated before release.
