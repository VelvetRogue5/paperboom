Paper Boom! for Android

Information Handled by the App

Paper Boom! does not require an account and does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, precise location, photos, contacts, microphone, camera, or payment card details.

To provide the game, Paper Boom! stores game state and preferences on your device. This may include your current level, session progress, coins, lives and timers, rocket inventory, streak and tutorial state, music, sound and haptic settings, purchase-related in-game inventory, and technical state needed to keep local saves consistent.

The app generates a random device identifier the first time it runs and stores it in the app's own storage. It is not derived from your identity, your Google Account, or the Android Advertising ID. This identifier is sent with the analytics, crash-reporting, advertising, and measurement data described below so that events from the same installation can be counted together. It is removed when you clear the app's storage or uninstall the app, unless Android restores the app's data from a backup.

The app also creates a separate random support identifier and stores it on your device. If you open support, the identifier may be placed in the subject of a draft email. It leaves your device only if you choose to send that email.

Along with the identifiers above, the services described below receive technical information about your device and the app: device model and manufacturer, Android version, screen size, app version and version code, language and region settings, time zone, store channel, network connection status, and the IP address your device uses to reach the internet.

Analytics and Crash Reporting

Paper Boom! uses Google Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics. Analytics records gameplay and app lifecycle events so we can see how the game is actually played and where players get stuck. Crashlytics records crashes and unhandled errors so we can fix them.

The events we send describe gameplay, not you personally. They cover:

  • App and session activity: app opens, session start and end, session length, screens viewed.
  • Level activity: level start, level end and result, retries, continues, and tutorial progress.
  • In-game economy: coins earned and spent, lives consumed, streaks, gift boxes, and booster usage.
  • Shop and purchase activity: shop opens, product taps, and purchase results.
  • Settings activity: settings opened, closed, and toggled.
  • Ad activity: ad requests, loads, impressions, failures, closes, and rewarded-ad completions.
  • Experiment activity: which configuration or A/B group your installation received.
  • Delivery diagnostics: whether our own events and reports reached our servers, and how late they were.

We also set a small number of properties about the installation, such as current level, last completed level, last session length, level pack version, a coarse coin-balance range, and the build channel and build number. Crash reports include the device identifier, device and OS details, app version, and a technical stack trace.

Google processes this data as our service provider under the Firebase privacy documentation and the Google Privacy Policy.

Advertising and the Advertising ID

Paper Boom! includes in-app advertising and is monetized primarily through ads. The app can show rewarded video ads that you choose to watch in exchange for an in-game reward, and interstitial ads that appear between levels. A banner format also exists in the app and is disabled by default; if we enable it, ads may also appear at the bottom of the gameplay screen. Ad placement, frequency, and cooldowns are controlled by remote configuration and can change without an app update.

Ads are delivered through AppLovin MAX, which mediates demand from several advertising networks. The networks currently integrated in the app are AppLovin, Unity Ads, Vungle (Liftoff), Mintegral, Pangle (ByteDance), BidMachine, and Bigo Ads. Depending on your version, some of these networks may not be enabled, but the software components ship with the app.

To select, deliver, and measure ads, these providers process device and usage information. This typically includes the Android Advertising ID, which their SDKs may access for advertising and measurement purposes, together with the app's own device identifier described above, IP address and the coarse location derived from it, device model and Android version, network and locale information, and your interactions with the ads shown. We do not send them your game saves or your support email. AppLovin also reports estimated ad revenue for each impression back to the app, which we use for the measurement described in the next section.

Each advertising network handles the data it receives as an independent controller under its own privacy policy, including AppLovin and Unity. AppLovin publishes the current list of its mediation and demand partners on its privacy page.

Install Measurement and Attribution

Paper Boom! reports installation and gameplay milestones to our own measurement endpoint at app-d.paperboom.com.ai, operated for us so that we can count installs, understand which marketing channel a player came from, and measure how much advertising revenue an installation generates in aggregate.

On first launch the app sends an install report containing the device identifier, app id, platform, app version and version code, store channel and region, device model and manufacturer, Android version, screen size, language, country, and time zone. After that, selected events — app opens, tutorial completion, ad impressions, and cumulative ad revenue — are sent with the same identifiers.

These reports are queued on your device when the network is unavailable and retried later, so a report may be delivered hours or days after the event happened. Queued reports are stored on your device until they are delivered or expire.

Remote Configuration and Experiments

The app uses Firebase Remote Config to fetch settings such as ad frequency, level pacing, and shop availability, and to run A/B tests. Fetching configuration sends the device identifier and basic app and device information to Google, and your installation may be assigned to a test group. The group you are in is recorded as an analytics event so results can be compared. If configuration cannot be fetched, the app falls back to the values built into the release.

Your Choices

  • Rewarded ads are optional. You choose whether to watch one. Interstitial ads are shown automatically between levels and cannot currently be turned off in the game.
  • Advertising ID controls. In Android Settings you can reset your Advertising ID, or delete it so apps receive no advertising identifier at all, under Settings › Privacy › Ads (the exact path varies by device).
  • Clearing app data. Clearing the app's storage or uninstalling Paper Boom! removes your local game saves and the app's device identifier. Because there is no cloud save, this also removes your progress permanently.
  • Requests about your data. Email us to ask about, or request deletion of, data associated with your device identifier. It helps if you include the support identifier from the in-game support email so we can find the right records.

Android Permissions and Haptics

Paper Boom! may use Android's vibration capability to provide optional haptic feedback for taps, impacts, warnings, and wins. This capability does not provide us with personal information. You can turn haptics off in the game's settings.

The app uses network access to load ads, fetch configuration, and send the reports described above. Bundled advertising components also declare the Advertising ID permission. The current version does not request access to precise location, contacts, photos, microphone, or camera.

Google Play Purchases

The current build reviewed for this policy does not include active Google Play purchase processing. If a future version offers optional in-app purchases, Google will process them through Google Play. Google may provide the app with the product identifier, transaction status, and purchase token needed to validate a transaction and grant an in-game item. We do not receive your full payment card details.

Google handles its purchase and account data under the Google Privacy Policy. Purchase-related in-game inventory may be stored locally on your device as part of your game save, and shop and purchase events are recorded in analytics as described above.

Support and External Links

When you choose Support, Paper Boom! can open an email app with a draft addressed to us. We receive only the information you decide to include and send, together with the support identifier in the subject. Your email provider processes the message under its own terms.

The app may also open legal pages in your browser, and tapping an ad will open a destination chosen by the advertiser. Once you leave the app, the privacy practices of the destination website and browser apply.

How Information Is Used

Local game data is used to resume progress, maintain balances and timers, and remember tutorials and settings. If purchases are added in a future release, local data may also record in-game content granted after a valid purchase.

Analytics, crash, advertising, configuration, and measurement data are used to operate and improve the game: to fix crashes and bugs, understand which levels are too hard or too easy, balance the in-game economy, deliver and cap ads, measure ad performance and revenue, run A/B tests, count installs and attribute them to a marketing channel, and detect invalid or fraudulent activity.

Information you voluntarily send to support is used to answer your request, troubleshoot issues, and protect the game and its players from abuse.

Sharing and Sale of Information

We do not sell or rent player personal information, and we do not share your game saves or support correspondence with advertisers. Data is shared only as described in this policy: with Google for analytics, crash reporting, and remote configuration; with AppLovin and the mediated advertising networks to deliver and measure ads; with our measurement endpoint to count installs and revenue; with Google to process any future Google Play purchase; when you choose to contact support; when required by law; or when necessary to protect rights, safety, and security.

Some privacy laws treat sharing advertising identifiers with advertising networks for personalized advertising as a "sale" or "share" of personal information. If you would like us to stop that sharing for your device, contact us at the address below with your device identifier.

Retention and Deletion

Local game data remains on your device until the app overwrites it, you clear the app's storage, or you uninstall the app. Paper Boom! currently does not provide cloud save, so clearing storage or uninstalling may permanently remove local progress. Queued measurement reports are held on your device for up to seven days and are discarded if they cannot be delivered.

Analytics, crash, and measurement data are retained by us and our providers only as long as needed for the purposes above, according to each provider's retention settings and applicable law, and are then deleted or aggregated so that they no longer identify a single installation.

Support emails are retained only as reasonably necessary to respond, keep support records, resolve disputes, and meet legal obligations. You may contact us to request deletion of information you previously provided through support, subject to applicable law.

Security

We use reasonable safeguards appropriate to the information handled by the app. Reports to our measurement endpoint are signed and sent over the network in the format that endpoint requires. No storage or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure, so avoid including sensitive personal information in support messages unless necessary.

Children's Privacy

Paper Boom! is a general-audience puzzle game. It is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The game has no accounts and no social features, but it does include advertising, gameplay analytics, and install measurement as described above, so it is not intended for use by children under the age at which consent is required in your country. If you believe a child has used the app or sent personal information through support, contact us so we can review and delete it where appropriate.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy when Paper Boom! or legal requirements change. The latest version will be posted here with a revised date. If a future release adds cloud saves, in-app purchases, or another data service, this policy will be updated before release.
