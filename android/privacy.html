Paper Boom! for Android

Information Handled by the App

Paper Boom! does not require an account and does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, precise location, photos, contacts, microphone, camera, or payment card details.

To provide the game, Paper Boom! stores game state and preferences on your device. This may include your current level, session progress, coins, lives and timers, rocket inventory, streak and tutorial state, music, sound and haptic settings, purchase-related in-game inventory, and technical state needed to keep local saves consistent.

The app also creates a random support identifier and stores it on your device. It is not based on your identity or Advertising ID. If you open support, the identifier may be placed in the subject of a draft email. It leaves your device only if you choose to send that email.

Analytics, Advertising, and Tracking

The current Android version does not use third-party analytics, crash-reporting, advertising, attribution, or remote-configuration services. It does not show banner, interstitial, or rewarded ads.

Paper Boom! does not access the Android Advertising ID and does not track you across apps or websites owned by other companies.

Android Permissions and Haptics

Paper Boom! may use Android's vibration capability to provide optional haptic feedback for taps, impacts, warnings, and wins. This capability does not provide us with personal information. You can turn haptics off in the game's settings.

The current version does not request access to precise location, contacts, photos, microphone, or camera.

Google Play Purchases

The current build reviewed for this policy does not include active Google Play purchase processing. If a future version offers optional in-app purchases, Google will process them through Google Play. Google may provide the app with the product identifier, transaction status, and purchase token needed to validate a transaction and grant an in-game item. We do not receive your full payment card details.

Google handles its purchase and account data under the Google Privacy Policy. Purchase-related in-game inventory may be stored locally on your device as part of your game save.

Support and External Links

When you choose Support, Paper Boom! can open an email app with a draft addressed to us. We receive only the information you decide to include and send, together with the support identifier in the subject. Your email provider processes the message under its own terms.

The app may also open legal pages in your browser. Once you leave the app, the privacy practices of the destination website and browser apply.

How Information Is Used

Local game data is used to resume progress, maintain balances and timers, and remember tutorials and settings. If purchases are added in a future release, local data may also record in-game content granted after a valid purchase. Information you voluntarily send to support is used to answer your request, troubleshoot issues, and protect the game and its players from abuse.

Sharing and Sale of Information

We do not sell or rent player personal information. The app does not automatically send gameplay data to advertising or analytics providers. Information would be shared only for a future Google Play purchase, when you choose to contact support, when required by law, or when necessary to protect rights, safety, and security.

Retention and Deletion

Local game data remains on your device until the app overwrites it, you clear the app's storage, or you uninstall the app. Paper Boom! currently does not provide cloud save, so clearing storage or uninstalling may permanently remove local progress.

Support emails are retained only as reasonably necessary to respond, keep support records, resolve disputes, and meet legal obligations. You may contact us to request deletion of information you previously provided through support, subject to applicable law.

Security

We use reasonable safeguards appropriate to the limited information handled by the app. No storage or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure, so avoid including sensitive personal information in support messages unless necessary.

Children's Privacy

Paper Boom! is a general-audience puzzle game and is not designed to collect personal information from children. The current version has no accounts, social features, behavioral advertising, or gameplay analytics. If you believe a child has sent personal information through support, contact us so we can review and delete it where appropriate.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy when Paper Boom! or legal requirements change. The latest version will be posted here with a revised date. If a future release adds cloud saves, analytics, advertising, or another data service, this policy will be updated before release.
