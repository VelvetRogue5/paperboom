Full Description

Step right up and make it boom!

Paper Boom! is a handcrafted physics puzzle game where every shot can bring the whole stage crashing down. Tap to fire the cannon, find the weakest point, and smash colorful towers before you run out of balls.

Each level builds a new paper-craft challenge from cans, boxes, ice, stones, jars, blockers, and moving platforms. Aim carefully, use gravity and chain reactions, and watch every impact scatter the stage in a satisfying burst.

GAME FEATURES
• 1,000 physics-based puzzle levels
• Simple one-tap cannon controls
• Colorful handcrafted paper-craft style
• Breakable cans, boxes, ice, stones, jars, and more
• Moving, rotating, and multi-stage layouts
• Limited-ball challenges that reward smart shots
• Rocket boosters for difficult structures
• Lives, coins, rewards, and win streaks
• No account required
• Free to play, supported by ads
• Optional rewarded videos for extra shots
• Progress stored locally on your device

Google Play Review Notes Draft

  • Account required: No.
  • In-app purchases: No active purchase processing in the current build.
  • User-generated content: No.
  • Player communication: No.
  • Contains ads: Yes. Rewarded video and interstitial ads via AppLovin MAX, mediating AppLovin, Unity Ads, Vungle (Liftoff), Mintegral, Pangle (ByteDance), BidMachine, and Bigo Ads. Banner format is present but disabled by default. The "Contains ads" label must be enabled on the store listing.
  • Third-party SDKs: AppLovin MAX and mediation adapters; Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics, and Remote Config.
  • Android permissions: Vibration is used only for optional haptic feedback and can be disabled in game settings. Internet access is required for ads, remote configuration, and reporting. Bundled ad components declare the AD_ID permission.
  • Data posture: Game progress and preferences are stored locally. Gameplay, ad, and crash events plus install and ad-revenue reports are sent with a device identifier. Support email is user-initiated.

Data Safety Draft

Draft answers for the Google Play Data safety form, to be confirmed against the production app bundle:

  • Device or other IDs — collected and shared. Purposes: Advertising or marketing, Analytics, App functionality, Fraud prevention. Includes the app-generated device identifier and, through the bundled ad SDKs, the Android Advertising ID.
  • App activity · App interactions and In-app search history (in-game actions) — collected and shared for Advertising or marketing, Analytics, and App functionality.
  • App info and performance · Crash logs and Diagnostics — collected for Analytics and App functionality.
  • Approximate location — advertising partners may derive coarse location from the IP address; the app itself requests no location permission.
  • Personal info · Email address — only if the player chooses to send a support email; used for App functionality (customer support).
  • Data is not encrypted in transit? No — all reporting endpoints use HTTPS. Data deletion: players may request deletion by email; uninstalling clears local data and the device identifier.
  • Not collected: name, precise location, contacts, photos, financial info, purchases (no active IAP in this build).

Data collection is not optional for the player, and the app is not designed for children, so the target-audience and content-rating answers must reflect in-app advertising.
