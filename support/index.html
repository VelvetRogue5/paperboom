Open Support Request

What to Include

Please include your app version, device model, operating-system version, level number, and a short description of the issue. Screenshots or screen recordings help when a visual effect, physics interaction, level state, or crash is involved. The in-game support button includes a random local support identifier in the draft email subject.

Common Gameplay Questions

A structure will not fall: try striking a lower support, changing the hit angle, or targeting a material that connects several pieces. Moving and rotating tables may require timing.

I ran out of balls: restart the stage and focus on weak points that create chain reactions. If a rocket booster is available, it can clear difficult parts of the structure.

Sound or haptics are missing: check the in-game music, sound, and haptic toggles, then check device volume, silent mode, and vibration settings.

Purchases and Refunds

Optional in-app purchases are processed by the store where you downloaded the game. On iOS, Remove Ads can be restored with Restore Purchases in the game's settings; coins, bundles, and other consumable items are stored with your local progress and cannot be restored after the app is deleted. For billing, purchase history, or refund requests, use Apple's purchase support or Google Play refund help. If a completed purchase is not reflected in the game, contact us with the platform, product, approximate purchase time, and store order reference—but never send full payment-card details.

Privacy Policies

Paper Boom! stores your progress locally and does not require an account. The game is supported by ads and uses analytics, crash reporting, remote configuration, and install measurement, which are described in full — along with the device identifier they use and how to make a data request — in the iOS Privacy Policy and the Android Privacy Policy.
