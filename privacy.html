Paper Boom! for iOS

Information Handled by the App

Paper Boom! does not require an account and does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, precise location, photos, contacts, microphone, camera, or payment card details.

To provide the game, Paper Boom! stores game state and preferences on your device. This may include your current level, session progress, coins, lives and life timers, rocket inventory, streak and tutorial state, music, sound and haptic settings, purchase-related in-game inventory, and technical state needed to keep local saves consistent.

The app also creates a random support identifier and stores it on your device. It is not based on your identity or advertising identifier. If you tap the in-game support button, this identifier is placed in the subject of a draft email so we can distinguish support requests. It leaves your device only if you choose to send that email.

Analytics, Advertising, and Tracking

The current version of Paper Boom! does not use third-party analytics, crash-reporting, advertising, attribution, or remote-configuration services. It does not show banner, interstitial, or rewarded ads.

Paper Boom! does not request Apple's App Tracking Transparency permission, does not access the Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA), and does not track you across apps or websites owned by other companies.

App Store Purchases

The current build reviewed for this policy does not include active App Store purchase processing. If a future version offers optional in-app purchases, they will be processed by Apple through the App Store. Apple may provide the app with the product identifier, transaction status, and purchase receipt needed to validate a transaction and grant an in-game item. We do not receive your full payment card details.

Apple handles its purchase and account data under the Apple Privacy Policy. Purchase-related in-game inventory may be stored locally on your device as part of your game save.

Support and External Links

When you tap Support, Paper Boom! opens your device's email app with a draft addressed to us. We receive only the information you decide to include and send, together with the support identifier in the subject. Your email provider processes the message under its own privacy terms.

The app may also open legal pages in your web browser. Once you leave the app, the privacy practices of the destination website and your browser apply.

How Information Is Used

Local game data is used only to provide game features: resume progress, maintain balances and timers, remember completed tutorials, restore settings, and, if purchases are added in a future release, grant purchased in-game content after a valid purchase.

Information you voluntarily send in a support email is used to answer your request, troubleshoot the issue, and protect the game and its players from abuse.

Sharing and Sale of Information

We do not sell or rent player personal information. The app does not automatically send gameplay data to advertising or analytics providers. Information is shared only when needed to process an optional App Store future App Store purchase, when you choose to contact support, when required by law, or when necessary to protect rights, safety, and security.

Retention and Deletion

Local game data remains on your device until the app overwrites it during normal gameplay or you delete the app and iOS removes its local data. Paper Boom! currently does not provide cloud save, so deleting the app may permanently remove local progress.

Support emails are retained only as reasonably necessary to respond, keep support records, resolve disputes, and meet legal obligations. You may contact us to request deletion of information you previously provided through support, subject to applicable law and any information we must retain.

Security

We use reasonable safeguards appropriate to the limited information handled by the app. No storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure, so please avoid including sensitive personal information in a support message unless it is necessary to resolve your request.

Children's Privacy

Paper Boom! is a general-audience puzzle game and is not designed to collect personal information from children. The current version has no account creation, social features, behavioral advertising, or gameplay analytics. If you believe a child has sent personal information to us through support, contact us so we can review and delete it where appropriate.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy when Paper Boom! changes or when legal requirements change. The latest version will be posted on this page, and the "Last updated" date will be revised. If a future release adds cloud saves, analytics, advertising, or another data service, this policy will be updated before that feature is released.

Questions or requests

Contact Us

For privacy questions or deletion requests, email contact@flowgames.net. If you voluntarily provide contact information, we will use it to respond to and manage your request. You may also visit the Paper Boom! support page.
